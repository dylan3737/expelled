    Quests

    Spend Energy on jobs. Earn gold and XP. Mastery ranks unlock at 5 / 15 / 40 completions. Avalon opens at Level 10, the Wastes at Level 20. Harder jobs need a large enough band or gear equipped.

    The Lists

    Spend Stamina to face rivals on the Field of Honor. Your odds depend on your attack against theirs; beating a stronger foe pays an upset bonus. Wounds cost Health — Rest at the Chapel when fallen.

    Chapel Rest

    Spend gold to mend wounds. Fights are locked while Health is 0.

    Bosses

    Great foes with vast HP. Each strike costs Stamina; the wounds you deal persist between strikes and heal only slowly. Slay them for great rewards, then wait for their return.

    Knight Band

    Knights who ride under your banner. Each raises your attack and defense in The Lists and against bosses. How many sworn knights you can lead grows with your level.

    Holdings

    Buy lands and towers. They gather gold over real time — Collect before the coffers overflow (2 cycles).

    Inventory

    Loot from quests, fights and bosses — Avalon and the Wastes drop stronger gear. Equip one weapon and one armor; relics grant passive bonuses while kept. Sell spares for gold.

    Collections

    Set pieces drop from quests in each region, from fight wins, and always from bosses. Duplicates are kept. Complete a set to redeem it once for a permanent bonus (uses one of each piece).

    Armory & Relics

    Relics are bought once. Training can be bought rank after rank, each rank costing more.

    Renown

    At Level 30 you may lay down everything and ride out again as a squire. Each new legend raises your Renown, which grants a title and permanent bonus gold and XP.
