Expelled

The rise of a dark magician

How to play

  1. Follow the story: your current chapter and its goal sit under your stats. Read the full tale in the Chronicle.
  2. Schemes spend Mana for gold and XP. Mana returns by itself (about 1 every 12 seconds). Start with Read Fortunes in the Gutter.
  3. Buy the Blackthorn Wand in the Sanctum as soon as you have 40 gold: +12 attack and +35% gold.
  4. Duels spend Will. Each rival shows your win chance; wins pay gold, XP and loot. If you fall, visit the Apothecary.
  5. Spells cost Mana too: hex a rival before a duel, quicken your schemes, raise a bone servant. Each grows stronger with use.
  6. Grow stronger: enthrall servants under Thralls, equip your hoard, buy Lairs that earn gold while you're away, and study in the Sanctum.
  7. Go further: the Witchwood opens at Level 10, the Ashen Wastes at 20, the Drowned Archive at 35 and the Black Spire at 45. Nemeses hunt you from Level 12, and at Level 30 you can be reborn for Infamy.

    Schemes

    Spend Mana on schemes for gold and XP. Mastery ranks unlock at 5 / 15 / 40 completions, each with a secret. The Witchwood opens at Level 10, the Ashen Wastes at 20, the Drowned Archive at 35, the Black Spire at 45. Darker schemes need enough thralls or the right gear.

    Spellbook

    Spend Mana to cast. Each spell rests a while before you can cast it again, and grows stronger the more you use it (Practiced at 10 casts, Adept at 30, Master at 100). New spells come with levels.

    The Dueling Circle

    Spend Will to duel rival magicians, hunters and worse. Your odds depend on your power against theirs; beating a stronger rival pays an upset bonus. Wounds cost Health; visit the Apothecary when you fall.

    The Apothecary

    Spend gold to mend wounds. Duels are closed to you while Health is 0.

    Nemeses

    Enemies who have learned your name. Each strike costs Will; the wounds you deal last between strikes and heal only slowly. Defeat them for great rewards, then wait for them to rise again.

    Thralls

    Servants living and dead who answer to you. Each raises your attack and defense in duels and against nemeses. How many you can bind grows with your level; lieutenants come on top.

    Lairs

    Buy hideouts, rackets and dark trades. They gather gold over real time; collect before the coffers overflow (2 cycles).

    Hoard

    Loot from schemes, duels and nemeses; darker regions turn up stronger things. Equip one focus and one set of vestments; talismans work while you keep them. Sell spares for gold.

    Grimoires

    Lost pages turn up in each region's schemes, in duel wins, and always from nemeses. Duplicates are kept. Gather a full grimoire to bind it once for a permanent bonus (uses one of each page).

    The Sanctum

    Instruments are bought once. Studies can be pursued rank after rank, each rank costing more.

    The Tale

    Your story so far, chapter by chapter. Each chapter's goal is shown under your stats.

    Infamy

    At Level 30 you may pour everything into a phylactery and wake in a new body, an apprentice again. Each rebirth raises your Infamy, which grants a title and permanent bonus gold and XP.

    Achievements

    Deeds the world will not forget, even through rebirth. Each pays its gold once.
